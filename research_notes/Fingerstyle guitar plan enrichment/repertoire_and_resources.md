# Graded repertoire path and learning resources for a 12-month adult beginner heading toward Kotaro Oshio / Sungha Jung fingerstyle

Research date: 2026-09-30. URL checks done with `curl -L` (browser user-agent) and WebFetch on that date. Sources are mostly retailer/publisher pages plus a few Japanese hobbyist blogs, which are flagged where used. Several things (exact Oshio tunings for most pieces, Sungha Jung's current official shop) could not be verified. They are listed under Gaps rather than guessed.

## Q1. Which Kotaro Oshio pieces are most approachable, and where are the official scores?

### Takeaway
Nearly all of Oshio's well-known pieces are intermediate or harder, and most need alternate tunings plus percussive slaps, tapping or harmonics. None suits months 1–9. The most realistic "dream piece" candidates for a month-10 experiment are his few standard-tuning ballads: 桜・咲くころ (Sakura Saku Koro), 木漏れ日 (Komorebi) and 風の彼方 (Kaze no Kanata), with 黄昏 (Twilight) as a stretch goal. Official scores still sell legitimately as Oshio-supervised Japanese downloads on @ELISE (¥200–¥800 each) and Piascore, and as Doremi TAB books. The Doremi special-page URL is now dead.

### Cited Findings
- A Japanese solo-guitar blog says most of Oshio's catalogue uses alternate tunings. It names 桜・咲くころ ("Cherry Blossoms Blooming") and 木漏れ日 ("Dappled Sunlight") as its beginner picks because both are in regular tuning. The same article points beginners to Nanzawa Daisuke's Ghibli arrangements and Kishibe Masaaki's slower open-tuning pieces as entry points. — [akogimania.com: beginner solo-guitar pieces](https://akogimania.com/beginner/etude)
- The TSG guitar school calls 風の彼方 one of Oshio's pieces that beginners can attempt, with a slow tempo. — [TSG guitar school](https://tsg-gt.com/info/6217/)
- 黄昏 (Twilight) is in regular tuning. Players on Yahoo Chiebukuro rate it harder than it looks because of its quiet, controlled touch, and harder than "Tico" for beginner-to-intermediate players (a community Q&A, so low authority). — [Yahoo! Chiebukuro Q&A](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q1138570661) (search-result summary; the page was not fully fetched)
- Sungha Jung's arrangement of Twilight on Musicnotes: $4.99, 7 pages, E minor, about 100 BPM, 4/4, an official "Signature Artist" edition "created with the artist". The page does not state a difficulty or tuning. — [Musicnotes: Twilight (Sungha Jung)](https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/sungha-jung/twilight/MN0228280)
- Tunings (low to high) from a fan-compiled Oshio tuning list. This is a hobbyist source, so confirm against the official score:
  - Christmas Rose: CGDGBD
  - 戦場のメリークリスマス (Merry Christmas Mr. Lawrence): DADGAC
  - Hard Rain (type D): GGDGGD
  - Tension: CGDGBD
  - Splash: DADGAD
  - Source: [ishiyamania-g blog tuning list](https://ishiyamania-g.seesaa.net/category/7156342-1.html)
- The same list, via search snippet, gives 渚 (Nagisa, with Jake Shimabukuro) as CGDGBD. — [search result referencing the same blog](https://ishiyamania-g.seesaa.net/category/7156342-1.html)
- Piascore sells Oshio scores with TAB and marks the grade:
  - 手のひら (Te no Hira): "中〜上級" (intermediate to advanced) solo guitar — [Piascore score 54257](https://store.piascore.com/scores/54257)
  - An Oshio Libertango arrangement: "中級" (intermediate). Its description says players with about 1–2 years' experience can manage it. — [Piascore score 20711](https://store.piascore.com/scores/20711)
- @ELISE runs an "Oshio Kotaro supervised official guitar score" feature with 81 songs from STARTING POINT through Hand to Hand (previously out-of-print scores reissued):
  - Downloads cost about ¥200–¥800. Convenience-store prints cost about ¥280–¥1,260.
  - Albums including Panorama, Color of Life, Nature Spirit, Tussie Mussie, Eternal Chain and Hand to Hand have separate "奏法解析" (technique analysis) guides for about ¥200–¥300.
  - Scores include a handwritten message from Oshio.
  - Christmas Rose, 渚 and 手のひら are listed at ¥400–¥800.
  - Source: [@ELISE Oshio official score feature](https://www.at-elise.com/Music/kotaro-oshio/)
- Doremi Music publishes printed TAB score books such as "TAB譜付スコア 押尾コータロー/10th Anniversary BEST [Ballade Side]" and "押尾コータロー/Encounter" (the latter co-credited to Nanzawa Daisuke). — [Amazon JP: 10th Anniversary BEST Ballade Side](https://www.amazon.co.jp/TAB%E8%AD%9C%E4%BB%98%E3%82%B9%E3%82%B3%E3%82%A2-%E6%8A%BC%E5%B0%BE%E3%82%B3%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BF%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC-10th-Anniversary-Ballade/dp/4285135191); [Amazon JP: Encounter](https://www.amazon.co.jp/TAB%E8%AD%9C%E4%BB%98%E3%82%B9%E3%82%B3%E3%82%A2-%E6%8A%BC%E5%B0%BE%E3%82%B3%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BF%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC-Encounter-%E5%8D%97%E6%BE%A4-%E5%A4%A7%E4%BB%8B/dp/4865713352)
- The Doremi "押尾コータロー オフィシャル・スコア配信" special page returned HTTP 404 on 2026-09-30. — [Doremi special page (dead)](https://www.doremi.co.jp/special/oshiokotaro/oshio.html)
- Yamaha's ぷりんと楽譜 (Print Gakufu) and Yamaha Sheet Music JP also sell Oshio scores. — [Print Gakufu search](https://www.print-gakufu.com/search/result/score___keyword__%E6%8A%BC%E5%B0%BE%20%E3%82%B3%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BF%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC/); [Yamaha Sheet Music JP](https://sheetmusic.jp.yamaha.com/products/2600001303117)
- Oshio's official site (kotaro-oshio.com) is live (HTTP 200) and has a MAGAZINE page listing guitar-magazine features, which may include score appearances. — [kotaro-oshio.com magazine page](https://www.kotaro-oshio.com/schedule/magazine/)
- Oshio's signature style uses open tunings, slap and tapping on steel-string acoustic. — [Japanese Wikipedia: 押尾コータロー](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%8A%BC%E5%B0%BE%E3%82%B3%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BF%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC)

### Inferences
- Oshio "dream piece" ladder, easiest to hardest. Ordering is my synthesis of the sources above:
  1. 桜・咲くころ or 木漏れ日: standard tuning, ballad.
  2. 風の彼方: slow.
  3. 黄昏 / Twilight: standard tuning, but touch and dynamics are demanding.
  4. Christmas Rose or 渚: CGDGBD open tuning, gentle.
  5. 戦場のメリークリスマス: DADGAC, harmonics.
  6. Hard Rain, Fight!, Splash, Tension: heavy percussion and tapping, alternate tunings. Flag all four as advanced and out of year-one scope.
- For month 10, pick one short section, such as the A section of 桜・咲くころ or Twilight, rather than a full piece.
- Doremi and @ELISE scores are in Japanese, but TAB is language-neutral. The tuning and technique notes (and @ELISE's 奏法解析 guides) need a translation app.
- For English speakers, the Musicnotes Twilight arrangement (Sungha Jung, $4.99) is the easiest legal route to an Oshio composition.

### Gaps
- Tunings for 風の街へ, Fight!, 月のカケラ, 黄昏 (only "regular" confirmed) and the standard-tuning claims could not be checked against official scores.
- I found no standard-tuning claims for 桜・咲くころ or 木漏れ日 beyond the one blog.
- I found no official difficulty grade for Twilight, Kaze no Machi e, Tsuki no Kakera or Fight!.
- Oshio instructional DVDs: the Sonic Academy "押尾コータローのテクニックポイント講座" course page appeared in search, but the domain would not resolve (DNS failure on 2026-09-30), so price and status are unknown. I could not verify any currently sold Oshio "ギター教室" DVD.
- I did not confirm whether Rittor Music currently publishes Oshio books.

## Q2. Which Sungha Jung arrangements suit a year-one learner, and where are his official TABs?

### Takeaway
The only live, verified official channel for Sungha Jung TAB is Musicnotes' Signature Artist catalogue: 112+ arrangements, about $4.99 each, with an "Easy Tab" filter. His Patreon page is removed. The sunghajung.com site sits behind a JavaScript cookie challenge and its shop could not be verified. MyMusicSheet now redirects to mymusic5.com, where /sunghajung returns 404. For year one, choose his earlier and simpler covers and originals over recent virtuosic work.

### Cited Findings
- Musicnotes has 112+ officially licensed Sungha Jung arrangements (guitar and ukulele TAB). The catalogue has an "Easy Tab" filter. Popular titles include Hotel California, Canon in E, Shape of My Heart, Sweet Child o' Mine, River Flows in You and My Heart Will Go On. — [Musicnotes Sungha Jung](https://www.musicnotes.com/sheet-music/artist/sungha-jung)
- A beginner guide recommends his earlier arrangements because recent originals are more technically demanding. It names Siesta, L'Atelier and Tadhana (UDD) as approachable, and warns off Racing Into The Night (tapping, fast repeats). Prerequisites it lists: arpeggios, legato, barre chords, glissando, thumb-slap percussion and natural harmonics. — [PlayFingerstyleGuitar: How to play like Sungha Jung](https://www.playfingerstyleguitar.com/how-to-play-like-sungha-jung)
- Songsterr hosts 50 Sungha Jung tabs, including Someone Like You, Howl's Moving Castle, River Flows In You, A Thousand Years, I'm Yours, Dust In The Wind, Let It Go and Remember Me (Coco). — [Songsterr Sungha Jung](https://www.songsterr.com/a/wsa/sungha-jung-tabs-a9680)
  - Caveat: Songsterr and UG tabs are mostly user transcriptions, not Jung-authorised editions (inferred from how the sites describe their catalogues).
- Status checks on 2026-09-30:
  - patreon.com/sunghajung and patreon.com/c/sunghajung both show "This page has been removed." — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/sunghajung)
  - https://sunghajung.com fails TLS/connection. http:// returns a JS "slowAES" cookie-challenge page, typical of free hosting, with no readable content. (curl check, no citable page)
  - The YouTube channels youtube.com/user/jwcfree and youtube.com/@sunghajung both return 200.
- Third-party sites such as classclef.com, gprotab.net and guitarprotabs.org offer free Jung files. They are not official or licensed. — [classclef](https://www.classclef.com/sungha-jung/)

### Inferences
- Suggested year-one Sungha Jung candidates (in this order):
  - Months 7–9: an Easy Tab Musicnotes title, or a slow ballad cover such as A Thousand Years or Canon in E, checking the preview first.
  - Month 9–10: Siesta or L'Atelier.
  - Month 10–12 stretch: Twilight (Oshio composition).
  - Mark Racing Into The Night and recent originals out of scope.
- Update the curriculum to state that Patreon is not available and to use Musicnotes as the official source.
- Do not add MyMusicSheet or sunghajung.com links until they have been checked by hand.

### Gaps
- Musicnotes difficulty ratings and exact titles under the "Easy Tab" filter were not visible to the fetch tool.
- I could not verify whether Jung's own tab books (Perfect Blue etc.) are still in print.
- I did not confirm which tunings Siesta and L'Atelier use.

## Q3. Stepping-stone pieces between Happy Birthday and Oshio (by month and level)

### Takeaway
Six String Fingerpicking already offers a numbered 1–8 difficulty scale (Novice to Expert) across 500+ songs, with free standard-tuning Level-1 tabs (Amazing Grace is Level 1 with a free PDF). It is the best backbone for a graded ladder. Japanese solo-guitar arrangements (Nanzawa Daisuke's Ghibli books, Kishibe's slow open-tuning pieces) make a natural bridge to Oshio.

### Cited Findings
- Six String Fingerpicking levels:
  - Its song archive grades pieces 1 Novice, 2 Beginner, 3 Beginner+, 4 Intermediate, 5 Intermediate+, 6 Advanced, 7 Advanced+, 8 Expert.
  - It has more than 500 songs.
  - It offers à la carte tabs (25% off for newsletter subscribers) and courses.
  - Source: [Six String Fingerpicking songs](https://sixstringfingerpicking.com/songs/)
- The Six String Fingerpicking Amazing Grace lesson is Level 1, standard tuning, with a free PDF tab. — [SSF Amazing Grace](https://sixstringfingerpicking.com/songs/amazing-grace-guitar-lesson-easy/)
- Nanzawa Daisuke's "Solo Guitar no Shirabe – Studio Ghibli" arrangements are described as relatively low difficulty with fewer notes, in regular or Drop D tuning, with a CD. Kishibe Masaaki's slower pieces introduce open tunings with simple fingering. — [akogimania](https://akogimania.com/beginner/etude)
- Dust in the Wind is suggested as a next step once arpeggio patterns are comfortable, and More Than Words is described as beginner-suitable. — [PlayFingerstyleGuitar](https://www.playfingerstyleguitar.com/how-to-play-like-sungha-jung) (search summary)
- Songsterr has a Sungha Jung "Dust In The Wind" tab. — [Songsterr](https://www.songsterr.com/a/wsa/sungha-jung-tabs-a9680)

### Inferences
Proposed ladder. Levels use the SSF 1–8 scale. Placements beyond the cited sources are my judgement and should be checked against each lesson's own level tag.

| Month | Piece | Level (approx.) | Tuning | Technique focus | Legal source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Happy Birthday | 1 | Standard | Melody + sparse bass | JustinGuitar Fingerstyle First Steps |
| 2–3 | Amazing Grace | 1 (SSF-rated) | Standard | 3/4, melody over bass | SSF (free PDF) |
| 3–4 | Greensleeves (beginner version) | 1–2 | Standard | Minor key, 3/4 | SSF |
| 4–5 | A Travis-picking tune, e.g. Freight Train (simplified) | 2–3 | Standard | Alternating thumb | JustinGuitar Folk Fingerstyle / SSF |
| 5–6 | Ghibli piece, e.g. Nanzawa's easier arrangement (Kaze no Toorimichi / "Path of the Wind") | 3 | Standard / Drop D | Sustained melody, Drop D bass | Nanzawa Ghibli book |
| 6–7 | Dust in the Wind (intro and verse) | 3–4 | Standard | Travis-style arpeggio, hammer-ons | Musicnotes / SSF / Songsterr |
| 7–8 | Pop ballad Easy Tab (Sungha Jung or SSF) | 3–4 | Standard | Chord-melody, harmonics | Musicnotes Easy Tab |
| 8–9 | A Kishibe slow open-tuning piece | 3–4 | Open (retune) | First alternate tuning | Kishibe score |
| 9 | Own 8–16 bar arrangement (folk tune or Happy Birthday reharm) | – | Standard | Arrangement | – |
| 10 | Dream-piece excerpt: 桜・咲くころ / 木漏れ日 / Twilight A section | 4–5 | Standard | Dynamics, sustained voicings | @ELISE, Musicnotes |
| 11–12 | Polish a 3-piece mini-set (e.g. Amazing Grace + Ghibli + Dust in the Wind or dream excerpt) | – | – | Performance, recording | – |

Flag as advanced or out of scope for year one: Blackbird (level 4+, needs a two-finger pinch at speed), Hard Rain, Fight!, Splash, Tension, Alip Ba Ta's percussive covers, and most Eddie van der Meer arrangements (many are intermediate-plus).

### Gaps
- I did not check exact SSF level numbers for Greensleeves, Freight Train, Dust in the Wind or Blackbird. The Greensleeves page loads (HTTP 200) but I did not extract its level.
- I found no citable difficulty data for Daniel Padim, Eddie van der Meer, Alip Ba Ta, Andrew Foster or Guitar Lessons 365 arrangements.
- I found no Reddit or UG difficulty-ranking thread in searches.
- Kishibe titles cited by the blog were given only in translated form ("Miracle Mountain", "Flowing Clouds", "Feeling the Morning Light"). Japanese titles and tunings are unconfirmed.

## Q4. Resources: costs, strengths, weaknesses, fit, and status of existing curriculum URLs

### Takeaway
Every existing curriculum URL is live except that justinguitar.com returns HTTP 403 to automated requests, most likely bot protection, so a human should check those five links in a browser. Add Musicnotes (official), @ELISE/Piascore (official Oshio), Transcribe! or Moises (slow-down tools), and SSF's numbered level system. Remove or annotate Sungha Jung's Patreon and MyMusicSheet.

### Cited Findings

  - thisisclassicalguitar.com beginner lessons

  - Price: $19.99/month or $149.99/year, per a 2026 aggregator. ### Inferences
- **Recommended stack:**
  - Free core, months 1–6: JustinGuitar + SSF free Level-1 and Level-2 tabs + a free metronome and tuner app.
  - From month 4: add Transcribe! (trial, then one-off purchase) or Moises Premium (about $36/year) for slowing down Sungha Jung and Oshio recordings.
  - For TAB, buy single Musicnotes and @ELISE scores (about $5 or ¥400–¥800 each) rather than subscribing to UG Pro ($99/year). A year-one learner needs only 6–10 arrangements.
  - Fender Play and Yousician are generalist tracks with weak fingerstyle-solo depth. They are low fit for this goal (my judgement, not cited).
- Map to months:
  - M1–3: JustinGuitar + SSF.
  - M4–6: JustinGuitar Folk Fingerstyle + SSF Level 2–3 + Transcribe!/Moises.
  - M7–9: Musicnotes Easy Tab + harmonics lesson + Nanzawa Ghibli book.
  - M10: @ELISE Oshio score or Musicnotes Twilight.
  - M11–12: recording with a phone and Moises/Transcribe! for self-review.
